The Fabric8 Method

Eight steps to partner success.

The operational backbone behind every Fabric8 uniform engagement.

Knowledge Allocation

Knowledge Allocation

We begin by listening: Your needs, pain-points, brand identity requirements, and operational environment are mapped before any solution is proposed.

Consultation

Consultation

Budget Management

Budget Management

We optimize quantity, item selection, and distribution to get maximum value within your approved budget and no surprises.

Order Deliberation

Branding

Manufacturing

Logistics & Delivery

After-Sales

Our relationship does not end at delivery. We handle guidance, problem solving, re-orders, and ongoing support for the lifecycle of your uniform planning.

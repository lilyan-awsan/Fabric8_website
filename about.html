About Fabric8

Our Story, Behind the Craft

B2B uniform solutions for clients who demand brand, purpose, performance, and reliability.

Our Mission

We aspire to be the most trusted uniform partner in the region; one that listens, advises, executes, and stands behind every garment we deliver. We bridge the knowledge gap between buyers and their business needs, so every client receives a uniform solution that works as hard as the people wearing it.

Our Vision

To become the regional benchmark for high-volume, high-quality uniform supply recognized for creative solutions, operational scale, and an unwavering commitment to client success over the long term.

Sustainability & Compliance

Fabric-8 is committed to responsible sourcing and operational integrity. We work with manufacturers who maintain compliance with applicable labor standards, occupational health and safety requirements, and environmental regulations. Our quality control process includes pre-shipment inspection and documentation review to ensure every delivery meets agreed specifications. We are committed to continuous improvement in our environmental footprint and encourage clients to consider sustainable fabric options where applicable.
